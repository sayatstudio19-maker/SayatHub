Our Dream

Two Girls.
One Beautiful Dream.

Long before the white coat, there is a dream. Safiya and Aayat dream of a day when they will stand beside people in their hardest moments and bring them comfort, courage and care.

Every page they study, every question they ask and every late night they stay committed is a small step toward that day. They do not dream only of becoming doctors. They dream of becoming the reason someone feels hope again.

“Some dreams are not just for ourselves. They are for everyone we will one day help.”
